StandardScaler([means, stds, replace_nan_token])

A StandardScaler normalizes the features of a dataset. When it is fit on a dataset, the StandardScaler learns the mean and standard deviation across the 0th axis. When transforming a dataset, the StandardScaler subtracts the means and divides by the standard deviations.
